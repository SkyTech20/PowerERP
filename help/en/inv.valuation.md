# Inventory valuation {#inv.valuation}

The value of the stock: quantity on hand × cost, by product and warehouse. Read-only.

**Where:** **Inventory > Inventory valuation**.

## Columns {#inv.valuation.columns}

- **Product** and **Description.** Click a line to open the product.
- **Category** and **Warehouse.**
- **Costing method:** *Moving average* or *Standard*, from the product (**Costs and prices** tab).
- **On hand:** quantity in stock, with the stock unit.
- **Unit cost:** the cost used. *Moving average*: the average cost. *Standard*: the standard cost when there is one, otherwise the average cost.
- **Value:** on hand × unit cost.
- **GL - inventory:** the inventory account of the product, for the accounting to come.

**Total value** at the bottom adds up the lines shown. The filter **Warehouse** shows one warehouse.

The value per lot, serial number and location is in [Stock on hand](#inv.stock).
